Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Which devices do you repair?

PS5, which is our specialty, Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, computer graphics cards and controllers. Another device? Message us on WhatsApp and ask.

My PS5 shows no picture on the TV. Can it be fixed?

It could be the HDMI port, the HDMI chip or another part of the board, and the PS5 is our specialty. Send us on WhatsApp the model (PS5 or PS5 Slim) and what happened: you get the quote before the repair.

How do I ask for a quote?

Send us on WhatsApp the device, the problem and, if you can, a photo or a video. Each type of repair above has a button with the message ready.

Where is Tech MeoW?

At Rua Sergio Silva, 50, in Engenho da Rainha, in the North Zone of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (ZIP 20766-670), near the Engenho da Rainha metro station. Message us on WhatsApp before coming.

Which Switch consoles can be unlocked?

Any Switch 1 model: V1, V2, Lite and OLED, for R$339.90 in the promotion. The Switch 2 cannot be unlocked yet.

After unlocking, can I use a PS5 controller on the Switch?

Yes, with Control PS-5, the free app we made: the DualSense connects over Bluetooth, with no adapter.

What is MeoWGuard and how do I buy it?

It is Tech MeoW's app for unlocked Switch consoles: it installs and backs up the games you bought, over the cable, on Windows, Mac and Linux. It is a one-time payment, by Pix or card, through InfinitePay, and access opens as soon as the payment is approved. See everything on the MeoWGuard page.

Is there an unlocking course?

Yes: the A Arte Kamikase course, for R$300.00 on Kiwify (in Portuguese). For people who already work with the Switch, there is also the unlocking and repair guide.

Which console is the interposer for?

The PS5 Slim that requires the Realtek RTD2175P HDMI chip. It takes the Nuvoton/Panasonic MN864739 and comes with the 1.8 V regulator already soldered, with no wires on the board. You convert the NOR for free with PS5 HDMI Tool.

Are the programs free?

PS5 HDMI Tool, Control PS-5, BancadaSync and JNX are free. PS-5 Controle Test is free in the browser and has an Android app. Iron Check is a subscription, with a trial key on WhatsApp. MeoWGuard is paid, with a one-time payment. The full list is in Projects.

Is Tech MeoW an authorized service center?

No. Tech MeoW is an independent repair shop, not affiliated with Sony, Nintendo, Valve, NVIDIA or Microsoft.
