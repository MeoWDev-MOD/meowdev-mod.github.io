Does it work on a Mac?

Yes. Over the cable, with the free OpenMTP app, or through the Chrome browser on one of our pages. The app shows which address to open.

What is NSZ and why does the Switch sometimes ask for the shortcut?

NSZ is a compressed game. To decompress it, the app needs a lot of memory. That is why it shows you how to create a shortcut on the Switch home screen, which opens with all the memory available.
